# Welcome to Blanco's lab
## About the lab
Blanco’s laboratory studies the biology of pediatric brain tumors with the goals of 
1. Identifying less toxic targeted therapeutics for medulloblastoma patients.
2. Studying the efficacy of novel regulators of the growth and propagation of very high-risk forms of pediatric brain tumors.
3. Targeting medulloblastoma stem cells to prevent cancer relapse.
